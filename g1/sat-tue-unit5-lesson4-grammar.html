🧸 this / that / these / those

English Mood Academy · Grade 1 · Unit 5 Lesson 4

this

Near, one thing.

هذا (قريب)

This is a pen. · This is a cat.

that

Far, one thing.

ذلك (بعيد)

That is a plane. · That is a tree.

these

Near, more than one.

هذه (جمع قريب)

These are my books. · These are cats.

those

Far, more than one.

تلك (جمع بعيد)

Those are birds. · Those are trees.
