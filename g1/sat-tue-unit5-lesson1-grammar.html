🧸 Adjectives · big / small · a / an / the

English Mood Academy · Grade 1 · Unit 5 Lesson 1

Subject + is / are + adjective

The adjective describes the noun.

الصفة كلمة تصف الاسم

The cow is big. · The chicken is small.

a / an / the

Here is a book.

Here is an apple.

Here is the sun.

It's + adjective

It's = It is

Look at the elephant. It's big.

Look at the ant. It's small.
