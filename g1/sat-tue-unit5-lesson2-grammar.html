🧸 Imperatives · Affirmative and Negative

English Mood Academy · Grade 1 · Unit 5 Lesson 2

Affirmative

Use the verb with no change.

الأمر المثبت: بنستخدم الفعل زي ما هو

Stand up, please. · Open your book.

Negative

Don't + verb

الأمر المنفي: Don't + الفعل

Don't talk in class. · Don't shout.
