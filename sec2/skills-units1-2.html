Unit 1 — Analytical · تحليلي

An analytical paragraph presents a clear claim, explains the reason behind it, supports it with evidence, and shows how the evidence proves the claim.

  1. 1 opinion · رأي
    claim · argument · judgement
    I think I believe my opinion
  2. 2 reason · سبب / شرح
    explain because that's why as since
  3. 3 evidence · دليل

    example

    For example For instance according to the text shows research · study

    fact

    research study article
  4. 4 explain evidence · شرح دليل
    this shows this means
  5. 5 conclusion · خاتمة
    overall to sum to conclude in conclusion
Unit 2 — Opinion Paragraph · رأي

An opinion paragraph gives a clear opinion and supports it with reasons and examples, then ends with a clear conclusion.

  1. 1 opinion · رأي
    Same first step as Analytical.
  2. 2 reason · سبب / شرح
    because سبب explain as since
  3. 3 evidence · دليل
    example   (same as Analytical · نفس)
  4. 4 conclusion · خاتمة
What's the same? Parts 1, 2 and 3 of an Opinion paragraph follow the Analytical paragraph (opinion → reason → evidence). What's different? An Opinion paragraph has 4 parts. It does not have a separate “explain evidence” step. Evidence is mainly an example.
Skills · شرح

Diary

A diary entry is a form of autobiographical writing. It is personal (feelings, experience, story) and is written on a regular basis.

Parts · أجزاء
  1. 1. opening
    Date · Salutation تحية · scene
  2. 2. story / event
    start · turning point · end / go on
  3. 3. reflection · درس مستفاد
    moral lesson
  4. 4. ending / conclusion
Benefit · فائدة

Psychological

  • reduce stress
  • safe outlet
  • self-reflection
  • clarity
  • creative growth
